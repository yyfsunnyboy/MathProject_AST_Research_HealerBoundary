\noindent Solve for $x$ in the equation: \[\frac{x}{4-\sqrt{7}} = 9.\] The value of $\textbf{radicand}$ is given as \(\mathbf{\texttt{7}}\).

Question Text: Find the rationalized denominator form where the numerator becomes an integer.
Correct Answer: $36 + 25\sqrt{7}$.
